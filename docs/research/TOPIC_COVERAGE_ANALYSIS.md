## Findings and limits

- The catalog has 15 question records linked to 7 topics. It has no item with encoded cognitive demand, and all catalog difficulty labels are `introductory` and uncalibrated.
- Every topic has only two or three items. Distinct objective IDs are not evidence of distinct conceptual coverage or reliable diagnosis.
- `expected-value-q1` and `expected-value-q3` share `expected-value-objective-1`. Their prompts use a fair-coin payout setup, so scenario/construct overlap requires expert review. This observation does not itself establish item dependence.
- Most topic coverage uses selected response. The one numeric question is in expected value. Whether the formats adequately sample the objective is unknown.
- The catalog is structurally complete, but this does not establish correctness, clarity, intended cognitive demand, distractor quality, difficulty, reliability, or validity.
- No missing objective-to-item link is asserted here. Topic/objective content sufficiency cannot be determined from ID counts alone.
- The previously completed offline evidence check found one local demo learner profile and two attempts; both canonical evidence links passed provenance checks, while six topics had no observations. This is a tiny descriptive trace, not independent validation. The existing two-distinct-question threshold remains unvalidated.
- Subjects/topics absent from this catalog are outside this report; no questions or curriculum have been invented to fill gaps.
